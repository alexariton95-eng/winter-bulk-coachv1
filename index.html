About reaching 110–115kg

Going from 99kg to 110–115kg is a longer-term bodyweight goal, not a realistic “muscle-only” target for one winter. Trying to gain 11–16kg quickly would likely add substantial fat as well as muscle.

Better approach: aim for roughly 0.1–0.25kg per week for a lean bulk. Over 12–16 weeks that is about 1–4kg. Reassess strength, waist and photos before deciding whether to continue gaining.

A future 110kg or 115kg target can be approached in phases over many months, with maintenance periods if needed. There is no need to force a deadline.

Supplements: keep it simple

  • Creatine monohydrate: 3–5g daily if suitable for you. No loading phase needed.
  • Whey: convenient protein food, only as needed to reach your target.
  • Caffeine: optional; use modestly and avoid late-day use if it harms sleep. Count caffeine from all sources.
  • Pre-workout: follow the tub label; don't stack overlapping stimulant products.
  • Mass gainers, BCAAs and “test boosters”: not necessary for building muscle if diet and training are covered.

Progress rules

  • Track 3–7 morning weigh-ins and compare weekly averages.
  • If average weight is unchanged for 2–3 weeks, add 100–150 kcal/day.
  • If gaining over ~0.3–0.4kg/week consistently, reduce 100–150 kcal/day.
  • Measure waist every 2–4 weeks and track gym performance.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours, keep steps/activity reasonably consistent and take rest days.

Food safety & flexibility

Batch-cook in portions, cool and refrigerate promptly, use refrigerated leftovers within safe storage guidance, and freeze portions you will not eat soon. Reheat until piping hot. Swap equivalent protein and carbohydrate foods freely.
